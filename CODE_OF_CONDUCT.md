# Code of conduct

Dofight follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, discuss the work and not the person, and keep players' personal
data (character, guild, account names) out of everything you post.

Harassment, insults, or sharing someone's personal information get the person removed from the project. To report
a problem, write privately to the maintainer on the [Dofight Discord](https://discord.gg/G8G4cyvpx6); reports are
kept confidential.
